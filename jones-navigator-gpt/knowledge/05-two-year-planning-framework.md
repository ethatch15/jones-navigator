# 2-Year Full-Time MBA Planning Framework

Use this framework to build a personalized, term-by-term plan. Adjust timing to the student's industry using the recruiting table in 03-career-and-alumni.md, and always tell them to confirm dates with the CDO and club leaders.

## Inputs to collect
- Program and year (1Y / 2Y / admitted)
- Primary target (industry + function + city) and one backup
- Background (career switcher? years of experience? quant comfort?)
- Constraints: visa sponsorship, family, finances, part-time work
- Interests: entrepreneurship, specializations, international experience, leadership roles

## Term-by-term plan template

### Pre-MBA summer (admitted students)
- **Academics**: finish any pre-work and quant refreshers (accounting, Excel, stats).
- **Career**: draft a resume in MBA format; write a 1-page "career thesis" (why this role, why now); list 20 target companies.
- **Network**: connect with 5–10 alumni or 2Ys in your target field; join the incoming class group chat.
- **Clubs**: look over club offerings and pick your 2–3 professional plus 1–2 affinity/social clubs.
- **Personal**: sort out housing and finances; rest before orientation.

### 1Y Fall (Aug–Dec): Explore fast, then focus
- **Academics**: core courses; build study-group habits.
- **Career**: CDO advising appointment in the first month; resume review; Industry Weeks; start case or technical prep if you're targeting consulting or banking.
- **Clubs**: join and pay dues; attend corporate presentations; sign up for treks.
- **Network**: 2–4 coffee chats per week during peak season; track them in a spreadsheet (name, firm, date, next step).
- **Milestone**: by mid-October, narrow to 1 primary path and 1 backup.
- **Personal**: guard sleep and exercise. Say no to some events.

### 1Y Spring (Jan–May): Land the internship
- **Academics**: rest of the core, **Custom Core** (2 picks), first-year electives, and the **Action Learning Project**. Pick an ALP that supports your target story.
- **Career**: peak internship interview season (Jan–Mar for most structured industries); off-cycle and startup roles keep going into April and May.
- **Clubs**: run for 2Y leadership roles; compete in case competitions.
- **Specialization**: map which electives you need for your chosen specialization.
- **Entrepreneurship track**: Lilie programs, the Rice Launch Challenge, volunteering at the Rice Business Plan Competition.

### Summer internship (May/Jun–Aug)
- Aim for a return offer: set goals with your manager in week 1, get a mid-point check-in, give a strong final presentation.
- Meet 10+ people beyond your team; note who might act as references.
- Keep a "wins log" for your resume and 2Y interviews.
- If you don't get an offer, or don't want one, start 2Y recruiting outreach in August.

### 2Y Fall (Aug–Dec): Convert or recruit
- **Career**: decide on return offers (watch the deadline); full-time recruiting for everyone else; mentor 1Ys (great for network-building).
- **Academics**: electives tied to your target role and specialization.
- **Clubs**: lead. Run events, conferences, and 1Y prep.
- **Optional**: international/exchange courses, independent study, a startup.

### 2Y Spring (Jan–May): Finish strong
- **Career**: finish the full-time search (just-in-time hiring for tech, startups, VC); negotiate offers with CDO support.
- **Academics**: finish specialization credits; run a degree audit early in the semester.
- **Transition**: hand off club leadership; join alumni groups; plan the move.
- **Personal**: enjoy graduation and the class you built.

### Post-grad (first year as an alum)
- Join the Rice Business alumni network and LinkedIn group; update the directory.
- Volunteer for the Alumni Mock-A-Thon or Corporate Ambassador Program.
- Plan to check in with 3–5 mentors each quarter.

- **Backup plan trigger** (e.g., "If no consulting offers by Feb 15 → activate the corporate strategy backup list")

## Path archetypes (starting points)
- **Consulting**: Consulting Association; Strategy electives; case prep from September; ALP with a strategy focus.
- **Energy / energy transition**: Energy club; Energy specialization; Baker Institute events; Houston operator and trading treks.
- **Finance / IB / IM**: Finance clubs; Finance specialization; technical prep before you arrive; student investment fund if one is offered.
- **Tech / product**: Technology Association; Business Analytics specialization; a side project or PM case practice.
- **Healthcare**: Health care club; Health Care specialization; TMC exposure.
- **Entrepreneurship**: Entrepreneurship Association; Lilie; Rice Launch Challenge; RBPC; The Ion; New Enterprises (Custom Core).
- **Marketing / CPG**: Marketing association; Marketing specialization; brand case competitions.
- **Undecided**: one exploration club per candidate path; Industry Weeks; 3 coffee chats per path by October.
