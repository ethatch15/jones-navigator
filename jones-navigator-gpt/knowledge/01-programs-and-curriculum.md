# Rice Business (Jones Graduate School of Business): Programs & Curriculum

_Last compiled: November 2026. Course lists change yearly; verify on the official pages below._

## MBA programs at Rice Business
- **Full-Time MBA**: 2 years, in person, at McNair Hall on Rice's main campus (6100 Main St, Houston, TX 77005).
- **Professional MBA**: evening program for working professionals.
- **Hybrid MBA**: mix of online and in-person weekends.
- **Executive MBA**: weekend format for experienced leaders.
- **Online MBA (MBA@Rice)**: fully online, with some in-person immersions.
- Joint and dual degrees are offered with other Rice schools (for example MD/MBA and engineering/science pairings). Check the official dual-degree page for the current list.

## Full-Time MBA structure
### Year 1: Core
The first year is mostly the **core curriculum** in the main functional areas of business.

Sample 1Y **Fall** core (from a recent program card):
- Strategy I
- Managerial Economics
- Data Analysis
- Negotiations
- Financial Accounting
- Organizational Behavior
- Finance
- Marketing

Sample 1Y **Spring** core:
- Strategy II
- Corporate Social Responsibility
- Operations Management
- Managerial Accounting
- Leading Change
- **Custom Core**: students pick 2 courses in the spring of 1Y. Recent options: Economic Environment of Business, Business-Government Relations, Business Law, Management Control, New Enterprises, Leading Across Differences.
- First-year electives

### Action Learning Project (ALP), MGMT 599
- Required 1Y spring consulting project.
- Student teams work with faculty and company liaisons on a real business problem and present their findings to senior management.
- Planning tip: ALP is an early chance to test an industry (energy, healthcare, tech, nonprofit). Choose your preference with your target career in mind, and use the work as a story for interviews and your resume.

### Year 2: Electives
- 2Y is almost all electives. Rice offers **100+ electives**.
- 2Y fall is also when most full-time recruiting happens for students who don't convert their internship offer.

### Specializations (Rice's name for concentrations)
- The Full-Time MBA offers **10 specializations**. Each takes roughly **9–12 credit hours**.
- Confirmed in current Rice materials: Accounting, Business Analytics, Energy, Entrepreneurship, Finance, Health Care, Leadership, Marketing (plus others; check the official page for the full current 10).
- Specializations are optional, and stacking more than one takes careful scheduling.
- Official list: https://business.rice.edu/rice-mba/full-time-mba/degree-concentrations
