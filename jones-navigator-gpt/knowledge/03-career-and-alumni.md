# Career Development & Alumni Network

_Last compiled: November 2026._

## Career Development Office (CDO)
- Website: https://cdo.business.rice.edu
- Phone: (713) 348-2622
- Location: McNair Hall, 6100 Main Street, Houston, TX 77005
- Services: one-on-one career advising (Full-Time students have a set number of personalized advising appointments; recent materials say five), resume and cover letter review, mock interviews, workshops, recruiting events, employer engagement, a job/internship posting platform, and curated industry resources.
- **Industry Weeks**: themed weeks for exploring careers (alumni and employer panels, workshops) by industry.
- **Signature programming**: https://cdo.business.rice.edu/signature-student-programming/
- Incoming-student resources: https://cdo.business.rice.edu/channels/incoming-students/
- MBA@Rice (online) students also get career advising, online workshops, networking, and curated resources: https://cdo.business.rice.edu/channels/mbarice-students/

## Alumni network
- **10,000+ Rice Business alumni in 54+ countries**, across Full-Time, Professional, Executive, Hybrid, and Online programs. Houston has the biggest concentration, especially in energy, consulting, finance, healthcare, and tech.
- **How to find alumni**
  1. **Rice Portal alumni directory**: the official directory (needs a Rice login).
  2. **LinkedIn alumni search**: go to the Rice University school page, open "Alumni," and filter by company, title, and location. Add "Jones" or "Rice Business" / "MBA" as keywords.
  3. **Rice Business alumni LinkedIn group**
  4. **Club alumni panels, treks, and conferences**
  5. **CDO alumni programs**
     - **Alumni Mock-A-Thon**: an annual event where students do one-on-one mock interviews with alumni (past participants worked at firms such as ExxonMobil, Amazon, Google, Deloitte, Accenture).
     - **Alumni in Residence**: alumni come back to campus to meet students.
     - **Corporate Ambassador Program**: alumni volunteers who act as points of contact at their companies.
  6. **Professors and staff**: ask for warm introductions.
- Alumni resources hub: https://cdo.business.rice.edu/channels/alumni/

## Outreach templates (the GPT should adapt these)
**LinkedIn connection note (<300 chars)**
> Hi [Name], I'm a [1Y/2Y] Rice MBA exploring [industry/role]. I saw you moved from [X] to [Y] at [Company] and would love 15–20 min to hear how you made that switch. Owl to Owl, I'd really appreciate it!

**Cold email**
> Subject: Rice MBA student, quick question about [Company/Role]
> Hi [Name], I'm a [year] in the Rice Business Full-Time MBA, interested in [role] at firms like [Company]. Your path from [background] stood out to me. Would you have 20 minutes in the next two weeks for a call? I'm happy to work around your schedule. Thank you, [Name] | [LinkedIn] | [Phone]

**Informational-interview questions**
1. What does a typical week look like in your role?
2. What surprised you most after joining [Company]?
3. What did you do during your MBA that best prepared you?
4. Which skills or experiences make an MBA candidate stand out to your team?
5. How does recruiting at [Company] work for Rice students (events, referrals, timing)?
6. Who else would you suggest I speak with?
Follow up within 24 hours with a thank-you, and update them once you have news.

## Recruiting timing (general MBA patterns; confirm with the CDO)
| Industry | Internship networking starts | Internship interviews | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consulting | Right away in 1Y Fall (Aug–Oct) | ~Jan | Case prep starts in fall. Use the Consulting Association's case partners. |
| Investment banking | Before or right at matriculation | Fall–Jan (often earlier) | Very early. Technicals plus networking. |
| Energy (operators, trading, energy finance) | 1Y Fall | Fall–Spring | Houston advantage. Many on-campus employers. |
| Tech (product, strategy, ops) | 1Y Fall | Jan–Apr | Many off-campus or rolling processes. |
| CPG / brand marketing | 1Y Fall | Jan–Feb | Often tied to case competitions and conferences. |
| Healthcare / life sciences | 1Y Fall | Jan–Apr | Texas Medical Center is next door. |
| Corporate finance / F500 rotational | 1Y Fall | Fall–Spring | Watch rotational program deadlines. |
| Startups / VC / entrepreneurship | Ongoing | Rolling, often spring/summer | Lilie, Rice Alliance, The Ion. Plan for "just-in-time" hiring. |

## International students
- Ask about CPT/OPT, STEM-designated options, and work authorization at the **Office of International Students & Scholars (OISS)**: https://oiss.rice.edu
- When building a target list, check which employers sponsor visas, and start networking early.
