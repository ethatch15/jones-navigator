## Student government
- **Rice Business Student Association** (Full-Time MBA): the official student association. It connects students with administration, faculty, and staff, and runs community events. Older materials call it the **Jones Student Association (JSA)**.
- Leadership roles (class reps, committee chairs) open each spring for the next year.

## How to choose clubs (advice for the GPT to give)
1. **1Y Fall: join 2–3 professional clubs** that match your top target and one backup. Pay dues early, because some recruiting resources are members-only.
2. **Join 1–2 affinity or social clubs** for community and wellbeing.
3. **Go deep, not wide.** Recruiters and classmates notice leadership more than membership.
4. **Run for leadership in 1Y spring.** 2Y officer roles (President, VP Careers, VP Alumni Relations, conference chair) are strong resume lines and alumni-network builders.
5. **Use clubs for alumni access.** Club alumni panels and treks are the warmest path to alumni at target firms.
