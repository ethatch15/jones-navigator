# Rice University & Houston Resources for MBA Students

_Last compiled: November 2026. Verify hours, eligibility, and links on rice.edu._

## Entrepreneurship & innovation
- **Lilie, the Liu Idea Lab for Innovation & Entrepreneurship**: Rice's campus home for entrepreneurship, based at Rice Business. Runs courses, programs, a summer venture studio, and the **H. Albert Napier Rice Launch Challenge** (an annual student startup competition with cash prizes; past top prizes have been around $50,000). Lilie marked its 10th anniversary in 2026. https://business.rice.edu/lilie-lab
- **Rice Alliance for Technology & Entrepreneurship**: runs the **Rice Business Plan Competition (RBPC)**, the world's largest and richest graduate-student startup competition (the 2026 competition was the 26th, with 42 graduate-led teams and $600K+ in prizes). MBA students can volunteer, judge-shadow, and network with VCs.
- **The Ion**: Rice's innovation hub in Midtown Houston, with startups, events, and corporate innovation programs.

## Leadership & policy
- **Doerr Institute for New Leaders**: free, evidence-based leadership coaching and development for Rice students, including graduate students.
- **Baker Institute for Public Policy**: public policy research center (energy, health, and economic policy). Good for events and research if you're interested in energy or policy.

## Academic & research support
- **Fondren Library**: databases (many business and market-research databases are available through the library), study rooms, research librarians, including a business librarian.
- **Center for Academic and Professional Communication (CAPC)**: writing and presentation coaching.

## Wellbeing & student life
- **Rice Wellbeing and Counseling Center**: counseling, crisis support, and wellness programs.
- **Student Health Services**
- **Recreation Center (Rice Rec)**: gym, pool, and classes.
- **Graduate Student Association (GSA)**: university-wide graduate student government, plus events across schools.
- **Office of International Students & Scholars (OISS)**: visas, CPT/OPT, and international student support. https://oiss.rice.edu
- **Disability Resource Center**: accommodations.
- **Rice Owls athletics**: student tickets, and a good social outing with classmates.

## Houston ecosystem (why it matters for planning)
- **Energy capital**: majors, oilfield services, utilities, trading houses, and energy-transition startups, many within a short drive.
- **Texas Medical Center**: the world's largest medical complex, right next to Rice. Healthcare strategy and operations roles, plus TMC Innovation.
- **Port of Houston and logistics**: operations and supply chain careers.
- **Fortune 500 headquarters, consulting offices, and a growing startup and VC scene** (Ion District, Greentown Labs Houston).
